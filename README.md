# Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights
Power BI dashboard project completed as part of Tata Group's Data Visualisation Job Simulation on Forage.

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of Tata Group's Data Visualisation Job Simulation on Forage.

The objective was to analyse an online retail dataset and create executive dashboards that answer key business questions through effective data visualisation and storytelling.

---

## Business Questions

1. How has revenue changed over time?
2. Which countries generate the highest revenue?
3. Who are the highest-value customers?
4. Which countries show the highest product demand?

---

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Data Visualisation
- Business Analytics
- Dashboard Design
- Data Storytelling

---

## Dashboard Highlights

### Revenue Trend
Shows monthly revenue trends to identify seasonality and business growth.

### Top Revenue Generating Countries
Compares revenue across the top-performing countries excluding the UK.

### Top Customers
Identifies customers contributing the highest revenue.

### Global Product Demand
Visualises product demand geographically across countries.
